# Mission 36: Hotel Z rooftop

The [Bulbapedia walkthrough](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough%3APok%C3%A9mon_Legends%3A_Z-A/Part_25) describes Team MZ joining AZ and Floette on the Hotel Z roof before going to Prism Tower. The [Serebii mission guide](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mainmissions/prismtower%27sdarkturn.shtml) describes the subsequent tower and rival encounter.

After the rank A reward, new mission 36 progress directs the player to the hotel elevator and rooftop before the tower. The AZ position and dialogue are a 2D adaptation, not verified original coordinates or a complete cutscene; the original wake-up and time transition are not reproduced. Saves already in mission 36 without the new requirement retain access to the prior tower route. This does not change existing battle rules.

The same walkthrough says the rival battle does not require a win. A completed defeat now advances to Vinnie's briefing without victory money or experience. The party is healed and remains at the tower as an adaptation to the existing turn-based system; explicitly forfeiting remains a retry rather than advancing.

The [Neoseeker mission walkthrough](https://www.neoseeker.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/Main_Mission_36) places the rooftop descent and the Mega Gengar/Mega Heracross encounter before the end of mission 36. The existing mission 37 first-wave encounter is now reached during that descent for newly advancing players, and victory completes mission 36 and moves the existing defense sequence to its second wave. Existing saves already in mission 37 keep their existing stage. The shared corridor, teleport onto the roof, enemy levels and sequential battle remain approximations; the original simultaneous battle and companion assistance are not reproduced. Leaving the corridor, losing and reconnecting retain a route back to the pending battle.

After the descent battle, Lida restores the whole party's HP, status and PP. The [walkthrough](https://www.neoseeker.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/Main_Mission_36) describes this recovery, although the precise recovery presentation is adapted to the existing game. It is applied only to the new pending mission 36 encounter and does not retroactively alter older mission 37 saves. Companion attacks during battle remain unimplemented.

Newly begun descents now have a distinct rooftop walkway and a player-operated connection down to the plaza before the Mega Gengar/Mega Heracross battle. The [mission list](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mission_%28Legends%3A_Z-A%29) confirms a rooftop descent, but the walkway shape, stair location and return route are 2D approximations. The transition to the roof after Vinnie's scene is still automatic, and Naveen and Lida do not independently follow or join combat. A save that was already descending retains its previous map. A new descent keeps its rooftop/plaza state across reentry.
