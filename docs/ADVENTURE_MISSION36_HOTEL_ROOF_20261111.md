# Mission 36: Hotel Z rooftop

The [Bulbapedia walkthrough](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough%3APok%C3%A9mon_Legends%3A_Z-A/Part_25) describes Team MZ joining AZ and Floette on the Hotel Z roof before going to Prism Tower. The [Serebii mission guide](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mainmissions/prismtower%27sdarkturn.shtml) describes the subsequent tower and rival encounter.

After the rank A reward, new mission 36 progress directs the player to the hotel elevator and rooftop before the tower. The AZ position and dialogue are a 2D adaptation, not verified original coordinates or a complete cutscene; the original wake-up and time transition are not reproduced. Saves already in mission 36 without the new requirement retain access to the prior tower route. This does not change existing battle rules.
