# Pokémon Research Lab: director's office

- The Director's Office is on the third floor, accessed using the lab elevator. Main Missions 35 and 40 direct the player to Mable there: [Mission 35](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-35-reaching-rank-a), [Mission 40](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-40-the-one-that-gives), [Side Mission 22](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/side-mission-022-a-call-from-mable).
- The lobby lift and separate office allow the story actions in the office. The former lobby interactions are retained so existing saves and previously learned routes still work.
- The office's tile coordinates, size, furniture and the elevator's precise position are provisional 2D approximations. The remaining original research lab floors and full interior layout are not implemented.
