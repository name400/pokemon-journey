# Mission 37 defense route draft

[Thonky's mission walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-37-operation-protect-lumiose) describes a park, Ivor's bridge and canal route, scaffolding, the subsequent building and the climb to rooftops in sequence. The former defense map put every encounter in one open room. New mission 36 completions now unlock a six-section 2D route in encounter order. Terrain colors and textures distinguish its sections; closed passages open as encounters finish, and the tower confrontation follows the final rooftop battle.

The room dimensions, exact coordinates, walls, passage gates, terrain textures and the return exit are approximations. This is not a reproduction of the original 3D scaffolding, elevation or companions' real-time actions. Existing mission 37 saves retain their former map and coordinates; the new layout is flagged only when the new mission 36 descent is completed. Exiting and returning to the new route starts at its entrance while preserving progress.

Newly started routes now include a switchback in the scaffolding section, reflecting the [walkthrough's](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-37-operation-protect-lumiose) description of a platform route and opening. The precise walls, steps, elevation and drop are still provisional. Route layout version 2 and earlier remain unchanged for existing saves, including positions on newly blocked tiles. Section labels identify the park, bridge, scaffolding, building and rooftop; they do not establish precise original geometry.

New routes now include a distinct lower canal-side walkway between the park battle and the bridge battle, reached by descending and ascending stairs. [The mission walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-37-operation-protect-lumiose) describes crossing the bridge, descending beside the canal and climbing again before the Steelix and Blastoise battle. Version 4 is selected only after a new Mission 36 descent; earlier layout versions and their stored coordinates stay intact. Water outline, stair positions and walkway proportions remain a 2D draft.

Layout 5 adds a separate building interior for the Gardevoir/Gallade battle and, after it, a human ladder to a separate rooftop route for the final three-Pokémon encounter and Zygarde. The [walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-37-operation-protect-lumiose) confirms this sequence, while the building and roof geometry, reverse ladder and precise positions are 2D drafts. New descents select this layout; saved layout 4 and earlier keep their existing routes, and the canal route remains available in layout 5.

New descents also enable a separate scaffold level in stage 4: climb up, navigate the platforms and descend through an opening before the Aggron/Salamence battle. The [walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-37-operation-protect-lumiose) confirms a scaffold ascent and opening followed by a drop; the exact walls, floor plan, ladder, height and return path are provisional 2D adaptations. No item is placed at the original platform yet. Existing mission 37 saves keep their prior layout, and the new scaffold position is saved across reconnection.

New descents now connect the final rooftop battle to a separate tower approach, with Zygarde visible ahead before the turn-based finale. The [walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-37-operation-protect-lumiose) confirms that the player follows Zygarde after the rooftop battle; this approach's shape and elevation are approximate, and Zygarde does not move independently. Existing mission 37 saves retain direct entry to the finale. The new route and the existing final-battle progress survive reconnection.

In the tower approach, a visual-only guide now moves along walkable tiles ahead of the player. Its position is calculated on the client from the player’s position and is not saved, interactive, or an autonomous simulation; collision and encounter decisions remain server-controlled. Existing and reduced-motion displays remain supported.

새 미션 36에서 로토글라이드를 이미 해금한 경우, 미션 37 비계의 별도 발판으로 건너가 풀회복약을 얻고 발판 끝에서 활공해 개구부 쪽으로 진행한다. [진행 안내](https://pokequery.com/games/legends-za/walkthrough)는 풀회복약 발판과 로토글라이드를 설명한다. 발판의 크기·좌표와 복귀 방법은 추정이다. 도구는 가방 한도 내에서 한 번만 수령하며, 기존 비계 저장과 로토글라이드 미해금 저장은 이전 경로를 유지한다.
