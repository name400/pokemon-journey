# Magenta Sector 1 and Café Gallant

[Magenta Sector 1](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Magenta_Sector_1) is between Centrico Plaza and Sector 3, next to Magenta Street and Estival Avenue; the Magenta Plaza Pokémon Center is in this sector. [Café Gallant](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Caf%C3%A9_Gallant) is just off Estival Avenue and Naveen gives the special galette there during Mission 14.

A separate street screen, four nearby screen links, Pokémon Center and café interior provide the story route. Existing draft galette interactions on Bleu Street and Centrico remain for earlier saves. Screen boundaries, café and center coordinates, road and building layout, café furnishings and Naveen's tile are provisional. The original café's drink menu, fast travel and a separate galette inventory item are not implemented; the existing story stage records that the galette was received.
