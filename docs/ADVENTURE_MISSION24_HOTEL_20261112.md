# Mission 24: Corbeau and Hotel Z

[The original mission account](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mainmissions/reachingrankc.shtml) describes Corbeau's Mega Scolipede, his overnight stay at Hotel Z and the tip that settles Team MZ's debt. New promotion battles give Scolipede its Scolipite. After a newly claimed promotion reward the player returns to the lobby, rests until the next morning in room 202, and hears about the settled debt in the lobby before the Mission 25 court battles. The exact dialogue and timing are an adaptation to the existing turn-based game and game clock. If the clock is already 8 a.m., the story sleep advances a full day. No player money is deducted: the debt belongs to Team MZ in the story.

The hotel stage is set only when the reward is newly claimed; saves that already advanced have no new prerequisite, and existing combat snapshots are unchanged. The standard long campaign fixture includes the new steps. Isolated API/engine tests cover Mega Evolution, loss, victory, reward, blocked early court access, both rest choices, next-morning timing, reload, old saves and old combat. For the victory path enemies were reduced to 1 HP and put to sleep, so normal difficulty is unverified. Original hardware and production were not tested.
