# Canari quiz at the electrical substation

[Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/mainmissions/reachingranke.shtml) documents the DYN4MO password, three true/false questions, the galette, Mani battle and final question. [Gamer.org](https://www.gamer.org/pokemon-legends-za-canari-quiz-answers-complete-guide/) places the event at the substation beside the Saison Canal and describes separate green and red answer areas.

A provisional entrance on the existing Magenta canal screen leads to a separate contest map. The player selects the physical true/false areas, returns from Café Gallant, battles Mani and answers the final question. The previous North Boulevard interaction remains for old saves. The map dimensions, exact door and stage positions, crowd and set design are not verified; the password is acknowledged in one interaction rather than selected word by word. The underlying timed/real-time original presentation remains adapted to the existing turn-based game.
