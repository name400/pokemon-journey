# Racine Construction and Rank E

[Racine Construction](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Racine_Construction) is in Vert Sector 6. Tarragon battles the player before guiding them to Canari's small room; Canari then leads them to the garage for the promotion battle. The [mission walkthrough](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/main-mission-14-reaching-rank-e) confirms the building and sequence.

The existing Sector 6 gains a draft entrance and separate garage and room interiors. The existing trainer battles and reward now work in the garage, with the room conversation required on this new route. The old construction-site interaction remains available for previous saves. The entrance coordinates, architecture, decorations, room-to-garage transition and staging are approximations; no original floor plan or visual comparison was available.
