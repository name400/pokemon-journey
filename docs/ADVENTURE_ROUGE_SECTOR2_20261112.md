# Rouge Sector 2 draft

Sources: [Rouge Sector 2](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rouge_Sector_2) locates the sector north of Prism Tower, southwest of Académie Étoile, between Rouge Street and Hibernal Avenue, and identifies Galerie de la Lune inside it. [Académie Étoile](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Acad%C3%A9mie_%C3%89toile) places the school north of the sector.

The additional 2D screen represents these neighborhood relationships and provides routes to the existing academy, two streets and gallery. The exact street grid, entrances, gallery access and building footprints are provisional; their positions and direct access from the school courtyard have not been verified from gameplay. The existing gallery and street exits remain available. Restaurant Le Wow and other sector facilities are not implemented by this change. The map needs comparison with accessible in-game visual evidence before its detailed layout can be considered accurate.

Restaurant Le Wow now has a draft entrance and interior; its tile position and room furnishings remain unverified. [The original side mission](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/sidemissions/fullcourseofbattles:threestars.shtml) unlocks after starting main mission 31 and has ten battles without healing between them. That course and its rewards have not yet been implemented; the attendant explicitly says so.
