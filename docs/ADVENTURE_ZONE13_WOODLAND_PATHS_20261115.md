# Zone 13 woodland paths

Poképédia describes a wooded stretch of North Boulevard with a west–east road,
some felled trees, and west, east and south entrances. Gamer Guides places
Heracross near the west entrance and Pinsir south of the road. These are
secondary sources, not a tile-level survey:

- https://www.pokepedia.fr/Zone_sauvage_n%C2%B0_13
- https://www.gamerguides.com/pokemon-legends-z-a/guide/wild-zones/north-boulevard-2/all-wild-zone-13-pokemon-locations/

The [fan-hosted overview map](https://github.com/Insektaure/Shiny-Stash-Live-Map/blob/main/romfs/lumiose.png)
was inspected for broad city context. It does not establish the precise forest
footpaths. The guide's detailed Zone 13 image returned HTTP 403 and was not
inspected. The new narrow paths around the two existing groves are therefore
an approximate 2D navigation aid, not confirmed original geometry. They reuse
only previously walkable bare tiles; grass encounters, gates, objects, trees,
and save coordinates are unchanged. A ground-level image or gameplay survey
is still needed to verify the terrain and exact route shape.
