# Z-A Royale bonus cards

Sources: [Bonus card](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bonus_card) and [Prize medal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Prize_medal). The original supports up to three active cards, many objectives, variable rewards, and nightly medal settlement.

The current game offers three simultaneously active cards in ranks Y–B: three super-effective hits (1,000 points and 50 medals), and two hits each with fire and water moves (200 points and 20 medals each). The positions, selection, availability and rank coverage are drafts. The common tracker counts successful direct HP damage once per move. Other types and card objectives, card replacement, trainer-provided cards and rank A are not yet implemented.

Medals earned from cards are saved independently of ticket points and capped at 9,999. During daytime the player may claim them for 10 coins each; this explicit claim safely retains unclaimed medals over reconnects and nights. This is an interim approximation: original automatic dawn settlement, trainer-victory multipliers, medal loss on defeat, trainer and field medal rewards, and the gold plush bonus are not implemented. Existing already-completed legacy cards do not retroactively grant medals, avoiding duplicate rewards. The current battle clock does not pause during fights as in the original. Pickup markers may remain visible after dawn, but pickup and progress are rejected during daytime.

The original single-card save field remains intact; the two additional cards and medal balance are optional world fields. The existing revision-guarded transaction saves card progress, score and medals together, and settlement updates the balance and wallet atomically. Representative checks cover simultaneous cards, wrong type, repeated hits, old saves, night rollover, API reload and duplicate requests, and settlement. Browser checks use a mocked API and do not establish a deployed Sites or production D1 result.
