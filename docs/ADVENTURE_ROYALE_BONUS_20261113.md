# Z-A Royale bonus cards

Sources: [Bonus card](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bonus_card) and [Prize medal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Prize_medal). The original supports up to three active cards, many objectives, variable rewards, and nightly medal settlement.

The current game offers up to three simultaneously active cards in ranks Y–B and, after Mission 39 begins, Infinite Royale (rank A): three super-effective hits (1,000 points and 50 medals), plus two type-specific cards (two direct hits, 200 points and 20 medals each). All 18 standard types are supported; two types rotate deterministically every game night. The positions, nine-night rotation, availability and rank coverage are drafts, not verified original random-selection rules. [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bonus_card) documents a two-hit type objective with this reward among other variations. The common tracker counts successful direct HP damage once per move. Other card objectives, explicit replacement of an active card, and trainer-provided cards are not yet implemented. Infinite Royale uses the same low-value variants as earlier ranks as a temporary choice; original rank-specific rarity and values are not established. Previously saved fire or water cards still progress until their original night expires even if the new rotation offers different types.

Medals earned from cards are saved independently of ticket points and capped at 9,999. At the first read or successful action after dawn (or a later night), pending medals are settled at 10 coins each using the documented win multipliers and the gold plush bonus; settlement waits for a running battle or capture window. A loss or forfeit against a Royale field trainer halves the medals. The automated settlement is lazy while the player is offline; legacy balances lacking a night identifier are retained until daytime. Three provisional field medal positions are available per represented battle zone each night: fixed awards use the lower end of the rank bands reported by [Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/z-aroyale.shtml), namely 10 for Z–V, 15 for F–B, and 20 for A. The precise locations, respawn behavior and within-band amounts are drafts. Each defeated field trainer awards medals once per zone per night; the provisional amount is 40 for one opponent, 50 for two, and 10 more for each further opponent. [Pokéos](https://www.pokeos.com/news/1231/Pok%C3%A9mon%2BLegends%3A%2BZ-A%2B%E2%80%93%2BBattle%2BZones%2Band%2BRoyale%2BZ-A) documents only the Z-rank one- and two-opponent examples; values for other ranks and larger teams are estimates. Limiting repeated trainers to once per zone per night is a project safeguard, not a verified original rule. Existing already-completed legacy cards do not retroactively grant medals, avoiding duplicate rewards. New adventure battles freeze the city clock across reconnects; pre-existing in-progress battles are not retroactively timed. See [combat clock](ADVENTURE_COMBAT_CLOCK_20261113.md). Pickup markers may remain visible after dawn, but pickup and progress are rejected during daytime.

The original single-card save field remains intact; the two additional cards and medal balance are optional world fields. The existing revision-guarded transaction saves card progress, score and medals together, and settlement updates the balance and wallet atomically. Representative checks cover simultaneous cards, wrong type, repeated hits, old saves, night rollover, API reload and duplicate requests, concurrent reads, wins and defeat, and settlement. Browser checks use a mocked API and do not establish a deployed Sites or production D1 result.

Infinite Royale card completion credits `zaInfinitePoints` with the same 50,000-point cap as trainer wins; it does not modify prior-rank scores. Unexpired cards retain their progress when the player advances to a higher rank; crediting the current rank on completion is a compatibility choice, not a verified original rule. The existing card save format and revision-guarded reward transaction are unchanged.

The active-card limit applies to incomplete cards; expired or completed cards do not consume a slot. The rotation uses the saved offset-aware Royale night, so it remains consistent across midnight, battle pauses and reconnects.

## Card replacement

[Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bonus_card) describes selecting a card to discard when a fourth card is found. A fourth draft pickup now offers another rotating type objective; when three cards are active, its dialogue lets the player choose one of them to replace. The discarded card retains its night and progress as a completed/discarded record, cannot pay its old reward or be picked up again that night, and does not consume an active slot. Other cards and existing scores and medals remain unchanged. The fourth marker position and deterministic selection are provisional, and the original game's exact card distribution and whether a discarded pickup can reappear are not established.

Representative unit and isolated SQLite API tests check the original and type-card replacements, invalid and stale requests, reload, score and medal preservation, rank transitions and walkable placement. PC and mobile Chromium checks use a mocked API. Actual Sites, production D1 and hardware remain unverified.
