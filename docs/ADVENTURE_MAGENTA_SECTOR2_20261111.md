# Magenta Sector 2 and Lysandre Café

[Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Magenta_Sector_2) places Sector 2 south of Sector 4, adjacent to Magenta Street and Autumnal Avenue; [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/pokemon/full-pokemon-legends-z-a-map-and-all-locations/) locates Lysandre Café east of Magenta Plaza. [Poképédia](https://www.pokepedia.fr/Zone_sauvage_n%C2%B0_7) describes the plaza's Wild Zone 7 and five entrances.

A separate draft street connects Sector 4, Autumnal Avenue and Zone 7 to the café. The 2D screen edges, fifth gate's precise placement, buildings, road shapes and café coordinates are estimates; the full Magenta Street and exact surrounding layout still require comparison with visual sources. The original street and lab return via Sector 4 remains available to preserve earlier saves, and the older construction-site route is unchanged. The existing rank gate applies to the added Zone 7 entrance.
